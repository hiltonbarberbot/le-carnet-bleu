# Operation Blue Cicada

A radio serial’s lost final episode contains orders for a sleeper agent.

## Fictional gathering

radio producer Armand Célier's private listening party for the final episode of The Blue Cicada brings five accomplished strangers to a house in Grambois. Each accepted a different private promise from Armand Célier; one came to make sure that promise could never be kept.

## Host

Armand Célier, radio producer and former resistance broadcaster; then Game Master

## Canonical solution

Hugo was the dormant agent. He understood the rehearsal line, silenced Armand during the listening break, and blamed the missing ending on professional sabotage. The guests’ independent memories show the script was a trap and that Hugo reacted to details no innocent actor had heard.

## Truth timeline

1. **The broadcast beneath the broadcast** — Armand built a real activation code into the radio script and invited everyone who might understand it.
2. **Dead air** — Hugo Bellac disabled Armand before the final line could identify him as the sleeper agent CICADA.
