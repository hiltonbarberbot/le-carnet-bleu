# The Cottage at Midnight

A private art restitution meeting conceals a map of postwar intelligence debts.

## Fictional gathering

collector Margot Bellier's confidential restitution meeting for a painting called Midnight Cottage brings five accomplished strangers to a house in Grambois. Each accepted a different private promise from Margot Bellier; one came to make sure that promise could never be kept.

## Host

Margot Bellier, collector and former courier; then Game Master

## Canonical solution

Olivier’s father sold the safe-house chain to the occupiers and later acquired the painting to suppress the record. Margot planned to return it with a decoded provenance. Olivier killed her and cast the dispute as competing ownership claims. The guests’ memories independently connect his family archive to the hidden map.

## Truth timeline

1. **A landscape of safe houses** — The painting records a chain of wartime safe houses in its altered details, including the real cottage in Grambois.
2. **Restitution denied** — Olivier Kern killed Margot because returning the work would expose his family’s betrayal of the network.
