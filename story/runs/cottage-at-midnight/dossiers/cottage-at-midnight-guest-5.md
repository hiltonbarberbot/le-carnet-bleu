_Art dealer_

Fluent, fashionable, and allergic to moral certainty

The host privately promised: Margot promised to clear you of knowingly selling looted work.

## Private identity

You discovered the false provenance and quietly brought the painting to Margot.

## Private objective

Save your gallery by proving you initiated restitution.

## Secret

You altered a sales date to buy time for the private meeting.

- **Shape the reckoning (3)** — Put your private objective before the table while keeping your own compromising secret from deciding the vote.
