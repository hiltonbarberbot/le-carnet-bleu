# La Colombe

A blue diamond, one dead patriarch, and two crimes committed by different hands.

## The gathering

August 1962. Sacha de Vernay has summoned five people to the House in Grambois and placed his family's blue diamond, La Colombe, beneath a glass dome. Each guest received a different private promise. Before Sacha explains why their lives meet here, he collapses over his cognac. The diamond disappears in the confusion.

The murderer and the thief are not the same person.

## Cast

- **Host:** Sacha de Vernay, collector and patriarch; after the staged death, the host becomes Game Master.
- **Hélène de Vernay:** Sacha's wife and the killer.
- **Renato Sabbatini:** film star, blackmail victim, and post-mortem intruder.
- **Poppy Ashcombe:** nightclub singer searching for her father.
- **Julien Bassot:** caretaker, boatman, and Sacha's unacknowledged son.
- **Odile Bassot:** housekeeper, keeper of the bloodline, and thief of La Colombe.

## Prepare

Use the app's setting checklist. The stable prop ledger is:

- `diamond-display` — replica diamond and dome
- `jacket-keys` — linen jacket and prop keys
- `document-case` — sealed inheritance letter and document case
- `framed-photograph` — harmless framed photograph
- `hollow-book` — hollow art book
- `bronze-dove` — hollow bronze dove and empty prop bottle
- `paper-envelopes` — Poppy's invitation and the other paper packets
- `safe-glass` — inert cognac glass

The glass contains nothing. Nobody drinks anything as part of play. Rehearse Sacha's no-contact collapse, keep every poolside action away from the water, and use the host as proxy for any movement a player cannot perform.

## The fifteen-minute opening

1. **Unveil La Colombe.** Sacha welcomes the guests, unveils `diamond-display`, and cues Poppy to read the `paper-envelopes` invitation.
2. **Bring in the rain.** Julien retrieves only `jacket-keys` from the verified poolside chair.
3. **Name the blackmail.** Renato briefly lifts `framed-photograph` and challenges Sacha.
4. **Put the promise in reach.** Odile opens `document-case` and places its sealed inheritance letter beside `diamond-display`.
5. **The last cognac.** Hélène places `safe-glass`. Sacha mimes a sip and performs the rehearsed collapse. The host secretly removes the replica diamond from `diamond-display`, reveals the empty dome, announces the death, and becomes Game Master.

After this, stop directing scenes. Open two hours of free investigation: ten tokens per suspect, five-token clues, free bargaining, and public accusations at any time.

## Canonical truth

Sacha was Julien's father. He meant to acknowledge him publicly and leave him the House in Grambois and La Colombe. Hélène had read the letter. She refused to watch property bought with Rachaud money pass to Odile's son, so she fictionally crushed Sacha's heart pills into his cognac, served it herself, and hid the empty prop bottle inside the hollow bronze dove.

Renato entered the study only after Sacha was dead, looking for the photographs with which Sacha had controlled him. His fingerprints and lies make him suspicious, not guilty.

Odile committed the second crime. In the confusion after the collapse, she took La Colombe and hid it inside the cottage's hollow art book so Julien would have one inheritance no lawyer could strip away.

**Hélène killed Sacha. Odile stole the diamond.**

## Reveal steps

1. Sacha's sealed letter and private promise identify Julien as his son and heir.
2. Poppy and Odile prove Hélène read the inheritance before dinner.
3. Renato and Julien place Hélène with both the cognac and Sacha's heart medicine.
4. Hélène and Poppy place Renato's study search after Sacha's death.
5. Poppy and Julien trace Odile's wet route to the hollow art book.
6. Odile and Renato prove the bronze dove was filled after six, separating Hélène's murder from Odile's theft.

## Purchasable clues

### Salon testimony

- The sealed letter's blotting impression includes “my son Julien”.
- The fictional glass residue is Sacha's own medicine, not anything Renato brought.
- A thread inside the bronze dove matches Hélène's glove.

### Cottage cache

- Blue velvet fibres and a damp apron mark connect La Colombe to the hollow art book.
- The study clock places Renato's search after the fatal glass was served.
