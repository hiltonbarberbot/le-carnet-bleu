# Hélène de Vernay

_Sacha's wife and the fortune behind the house_

## Public face

Controlled, patrician, and lethal with a courtesy. Rachaud money bought the house, boat, cars, and most of Sacha's collection; the Vernay name merely signed the invitations.

Sacha promised that tonight's announcement would finally settle what the marriage owed to your fortune.

## The truth

You killed Sacha. You read that he meant to leave the House in Grambois and La Colombe to Julien, Odile's son. You fictionally crushed Sacha's heart pills into his cognac and hid the empty prop bottle inside the hollow bronze dove.

You did not take the diamond.

## What you know

- After Sacha collapsed, you saw Renato leave the study white-faced and wipe his fingers on a handkerchief.
- Odile threatened to put the truth about Julien before every guest if Sacha failed him again.

## Objectives

- **Erase the inheritance (1):** Gain control of the sealed letter and persuade someone it should remain private or be destroyed.
- **Build the actor (2):** Make two suspects acknowledge that Renato entered the study and touched Sacha's glass.
- **Own the room (3):** Avoid conviction and make someone say the house was bought with your family's money.

## Opening cue

After Odile places the sealed letter beside La Colombe, put the inert glass beside Sacha without touching him and say: “Drink to the house you are so eager to give away.”
