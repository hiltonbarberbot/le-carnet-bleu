# Odile Bassot

_Housekeeper, keeper of the bloodline, and thief of La Colombe_

## Public face

Unsparing, observant, and almost invisible to anyone who mistakes service for ignorance. You have run this house for thirty years.

## The truth

Sacha is Julien's father. After Sacha collapsed, you took La Colombe and hid it inside the cottage's hollow art book so Julien would have one inheritance lawyers could not strip away.

You stole the diamond. You did not kill Sacha.

## What you know

- You witnessed Sacha seal the letter naming Julien as his son and heir.
- You found Hélène reading that letter before dinner.
- You polished the hollow bronze dove at six; it was empty and silent.
- You posted money orders to Poppy's mother and know Sacha was not Poppy's father.

## Objectives

- **Make paper speak (1):** Get another suspect to read the inheritance letter aloud.
- **Protect the son (2):** Prevent Julien's conviction, even if you must admit the theft.
- **Choose the inheritance (3):** End with control of La Colombe's location or surrender it directly to Julien after naming his father.

## Opening cue

After Renato returns the photograph, use the prepared prop key to open the document case. Place the sealed letter beside La Colombe and tell Sacha: “Paper is the only promise this house will believe.”
