# Julien Bassot

_Boatman, caretaker, and unacknowledged son_

## Public face

Proud, practical, and done being summoned by people who never learned where you sleep. Sacha refused your request for eleven thousand francs, then ordered you to dinner as a guest for the first time.

## The truth

You were born in the room behind the kitchen and have never been told who your father was. At four you shouted that Sacha would regret keeping you trapped here. He then told you privately: “At dinner I will give you the house, the Dove, and the name I denied you.”

## What you know

- Sacha's document-case key is on the ring inside his wet linen jacket.
- Hélène asked Odile where Sacha kept his spare heart pills.
- Only you and Poppy know the cottage's hollow art book. After the collapse, La Colombe was inside it, wrapped in Odile's polishing cloth.

## Objectives

- **Hear the announcement (1):** Use the keys to have the inheritance letter opened and read aloud.
- **End the silence (2):** Make Odile name your father before a witness.
- **Separate blood from theft (3):** Correctly identify both killer and thief.

## Opening cue

After Poppy confronts Sacha, walk by the host-approved route to the prepared poolside chair. Bring back only the linen jacket and prop keys, place them beside the dome, and tell Sacha you are done asking for what he owes you.
