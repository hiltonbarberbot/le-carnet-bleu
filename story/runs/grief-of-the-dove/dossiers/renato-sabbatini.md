# Renato Sabbatini

_International film star and captive ornament_

## Public face

Magnetic, sardonic, and exhausted by being looked at. Sacha promised to return the Portofino photographs he had used to control eleven summers of your life.

## The truth

You have loved Prince Amaury for six years. Exposure could destroy both lives. After Sacha collapsed, you entered the study to steal the photographs. He was already dead. You searched the desk, found a drawer already disturbed, touched the cognac glass, and fled.

## What you know

- Before dinner you passed Hélène carrying Sacha's cognac from underneath like a waiter.
- The bronze dove was silent at six. After the collapse, it rattled.

## Objectives

- **Recover the photograph (1):** Gain possession of it or convince someone to keep its subject private.
- **Control the confession (2):** Admit entering the study before Hélène turns the fact into a complete case.
- **Keep one name clean (3):** Reach the vote without anyone publicly naming Amaury.

## Opening cue

After Julien brings in the jacket, lift the prepared framed photograph, keep its image facing you, and tell Sacha that blackmail is not hospitality. Return it when the host cues you.
