# Poppy Ashcombe

_English nightclub singer searching for her father_

## Public face

Young, unsentimental, and quicker than anyone rich enough to underestimate you. Sacha wrote to your Paris club: “I knew your mother in Rouen. There are things you are owed.”

## The truth

You came believing Sacha might be your father. This afternoon you stole a photograph of your mother from his desk. It is yours by right, but it is still stolen property in a house missing a diamond.

## What you know

- You heard Hélène tell Sacha: “My money bought this house. I will not be written out of it for Odile's boy.”
- After the collapse, the study lamp switched on while Renato was absent.
- In the confusion, Odile crossed the wet terrace toward the cottage holding a small hard object inside her apron.

## Objectives

- **Collect what is owed (1):** Make someone explain Sacha's promise.
- **Refuse the easy suspect (2):** Break one accusation against Julien with a truthful fact.
- **Make the house answer (3):** Get the inheritance letter read aloud and obtain an answer about your mother.

## Opening cue

When Sacha unveils La Colombe, read your invitation aloud and ask whether he was your father.
