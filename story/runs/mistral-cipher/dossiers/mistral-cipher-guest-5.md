# René Cassin

_Aviation industrialist_

## Public face

Expansive, patriotic, and accustomed to applause

## Invitation

You were invited to test pilot Émile Vauban's memorial dinner for the tenth anniversary of the vanished Mistral flight in your public capacity as aviation industrialist.

The host privately promised: Émile promised to sign a statement preserving your company’s role in the Mistral legend.

## Private identity

You were the foreign buyer’s courier and profited from the secret landing.

## Private objective

Keep the landing deniable and shift the vote toward Graham.

## Secret

You killed Émile to stop him identifying you as the courier.

## Objectives

- **Collect the promise (1)** — Get another suspect to acknowledge what the host privately promised you: Émile promised to sign a statement preserving your company’s role in the Mistral legend.
- **Gain useful leverage (2)** — Learn why Thérèse Vidal is concealing evidence and trade that knowledge for a clue, token, or public concession.
- **Escape the reckoning (3)** — Keep the final conviction away from you without inventing evidence.

## Relationships

- **mistral-cipher-guest-1** — Thérèse Vidal can confirm part of your cover story, but only by exposing their own.
- **mistral-cipher-guest-4** — Miles Graham once helped your career and now expects repayment.

## Evidence

- You saw Thérèse return the missing fuel page and knew it could prove the landing.

## Opening cue

- **When the host calls on René Cassin during the opening:** Propose a toast to Émile as the only survivor and insist the crash story must remain untouched.
