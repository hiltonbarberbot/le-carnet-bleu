# Isabelle Dumas

_Navigator’s daughter_

## Public face

Composed, scholarly, and fiercely literal

## Invitation

You were invited to test pilot Émile Vauban's memorial dinner for the tenth anniversary of the vanished Mistral flight in your public capacity as navigator’s daughter.

The host privately promised: Émile promised to tell you where your father spent his final night.

## Private identity

Your father survived the landing and died years later under another name.

## Private objective

Recover your father’s true history without exposing his second family.

## Secret

You carry coordinates proving the landing but concealed their source.

## Objectives

- **Collect the promise (1)** — Get another suspect to acknowledge what the host privately promised you: Émile promised to tell you where your father spent his final night.
- **Gain useful leverage (2)** — Learn why Miles Graham is concealing evidence and trade that knowledge for a clue, token, or public concession.
- **Shape the reckoning (3)** — Put your private objective before the table while keeping your own compromising secret from deciding the vote.

## Relationships

- **mistral-cipher-guest-4** — Miles Graham can confirm part of your cover story, but only by exposing their own.
- **mistral-cipher-guest-2** — André Masson once helped your career and now expects repayment.

## Evidence

- You saw intelligence officer Graham pass René a weather strip matching the night of the flight.

## Opening cue

- **When the host calls on Isabelle Dumas during the opening:** Correct the memorial date and say your father was alive three days after the supposed crash.
