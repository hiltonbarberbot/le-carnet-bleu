# The Mistral Cipher

An aviator’s memorial dinner hides the route of a missing reconnaissance flight.

## Fictional gathering

test pilot Émile Vauban's memorial dinner for the tenth anniversary of the vanished Mistral flight brings five accomplished strangers to a house in Grambois. Each accepted a different private promise from Émile Vauban; one came to make sure that promise could never be kept.

## Host

Émile Vauban, celebrated test pilot and sole public survivor; then Game Master

## Canonical solution

René brokered the secret landing and built his career on the myth of the crash. Émile convened the memorial to correct the record. René killed him during the commemorative toast and tried to make the dispute look like rivalry between pilot and mechanic. Five private accounts reconstruct the landing and René’s foreknowledge.

## Truth timeline

1. **The flight that landed** — The Mistral aircraft did not crash; it landed secretly and delivered reconnaissance film to a foreign buyer.
2. **The survivor’s last correction** — René Cassin killed Émile because Émile planned to reveal René as the buyer’s courier.
