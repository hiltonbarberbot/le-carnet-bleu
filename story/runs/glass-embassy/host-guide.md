# The Glass Embassy

A perfume launch masks an auction for the identity of a vanished double agent.

## Fictional gathering

Madame Sabine Vautrin's private unveiling of a Riviera perfume called Ambassade de Verre brings five accomplished strangers to a house in Grambois. Each accepted a different private promise from Sabine Vautrin; one came to make sure that promise could never be kept.

## Host

Sabine Vautrin, perfumer and intelligence broker; then Game Master

## Canonical solution

Étienne was SABLE. He altered Sabine’s drink during the perfume toast, then encouraged everyone to treat the coded formula as a commercial dispute. Sabine’s separate promises explain every suspect’s secrecy; the overlapping memories prove Étienne alone understood the code before her death.

## Truth timeline

1. **The formula with two meanings** — Sabine encoded the name of double agent SABLE in the perfume formula and promised five different people control of it.
2. **A toast that silenced the broker** — Étienne Marot recognized that SABLE was his own handler identity and poisoned Sabine before she could name him.
