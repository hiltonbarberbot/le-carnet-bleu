# Marc Delon

_Formula chemist_

## Public face

Exact, wounded, and contemptuous of glamour

## Invitation

You were invited to Madame Sabine Vautrin's private unveiling of a Riviera perfume called Ambassade de Verre in your public capacity as formula chemist.

The host privately promised: Sabine promised to restore your authorship credit on Ambassade de Verre.

## Private identity

You discovered the formula carries a readable intelligence acrostic.

## Private objective

Recover credit for your work without being accused of creating the code.

## Secret

You diluted a sample to embarrass Sabine, giving you a reason to conceal your kitchen visit.

## Objectives

- **Collect the promise (1)** — Get another suspect to acknowledge what the host privately promised you: Sabine promised to restore your authorship credit on Ambassade de Verre.
- **Gain useful leverage (2)** — Learn why Lucien Arbaud is concealing evidence and trade that knowledge for a clue, token, or public concession.
- **Shape the reckoning (3)** — Put your private objective before the table while keeping your own compromising secret from deciding the vote.

## Relationships

- **glass-embassy-guest-5** — Lucien Arbaud can confirm part of your cover story, but only by exposing their own.
- **glass-embassy-guest-3** — Nadia Petrov once helped your career and now expects repayment.

## Evidence

- In the poolside parlor, you heard Lucien accuse Sabine of preparing to expose Étienne by his old call sign.

## Opening cue

- **When the host calls on Marc Delon during the opening:** Announce that the celebrated formula is incomplete and only Sabine knows the missing line.
