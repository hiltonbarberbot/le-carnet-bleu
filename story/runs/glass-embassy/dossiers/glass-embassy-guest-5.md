# Lucien Arbaud

_Retired naval courier_

## Public face

Genial, weathered, and selectively deaf

## Invitation

You were invited to Madame Sabine Vautrin's private unveiling of a Riviera perfume called Ambassade de Verre in your public capacity as retired naval courier.

The host privately promised: Sabine promised to clear your name in the loss of a diplomatic pouch.

## Private identity

You carried the original SABLE file and recognized its cipher in the perfume notes.

## Private objective

Prove the pouch was deliberately sacrificed by someone at the gathering.

## Secret

You warned Étienne that Sabine knew SABLE’s identity, hoping to force a confession.

## Objectives

- **Collect the promise (1)** — Get another suspect to acknowledge what the host privately promised you: Sabine promised to clear your name in the loss of a diplomatic pouch.
- **Gain useful leverage (2)** — Learn why Colette Renaud is concealing evidence and trade that knowledge for a clue, token, or public concession.
- **Shape the reckoning (3)** — Put your private objective before the table while keeping your own compromising secret from deciding the vote.

## Relationships

- **glass-embassy-guest-1** — Colette Renaud can confirm part of your cover story, but only by exposing their own.
- **glass-embassy-guest-4** — Marc Delon once helped your career and now expects repayment.

## Evidence

- You saw Colette conceal a formula page whose margin proves Sabine added the SABLE acrostic only after meeting Étienne.

## Opening cue

- **When the host calls on Lucien Arbaud during the opening:** Offer a toast to old secrets and say that one of Sabine’s formulas is really a confession.
