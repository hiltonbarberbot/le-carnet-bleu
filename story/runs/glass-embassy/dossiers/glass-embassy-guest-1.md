# Colette Renaud

_Perfume journalist_

## Public face

Incisive, amused, and always two questions ahead

## Invitation

You were invited to Madame Sabine Vautrin's private unveiling of a Riviera perfume called Ambassade de Verre in your public capacity as perfume journalist.

The host privately promised: Sabine promised you the true story behind her most famous formula.

## Private identity

You once traded an embassy rumour for access and have spent years burying the source.

## Private objective

Publish the truth without exposing the source you betrayed.

## Secret

You stole a page of Sabine’s formula notes because it contains your old source mark.

## Objectives

- **Collect the promise (1)** — Get another suspect to acknowledge what the host privately promised you: Sabine promised you the true story behind her most famous formula.
- **Gain useful leverage (2)** — Learn why Étienne Marot is concealing evidence and trade that knowledge for a clue, token, or public concession.
- **Shape the reckoning (3)** — Put your private objective before the table while keeping your own compromising secret from deciding the vote.

## Relationships

- **glass-embassy-guest-2** — Étienne Marot can confirm part of your cover story, but only by exposing their own.
- **glass-embassy-guest-5** — Lucien Arbaud once helped your career and now expects repayment.

## Evidence

- Before the unveiling, you heard Lucien tell Étienne that Sabine had promised to name SABLE before midnight.

## Opening cue

- **When the host calls on Colette Renaud during the opening:** State that Sabine invited the press for a perfume story, then ask why diplomats were included.
