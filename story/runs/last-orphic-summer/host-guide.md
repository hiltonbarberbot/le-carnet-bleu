# The Last Summer of Orphée

A composer’s listening party hides the extraction signal for a defecting cryptographer.

## Fictional gathering

composer Laurent Orphée's private first performance of his final suite, The Last Summer brings five accomplished strangers to a house in Grambois. Each accepted a different private promise from Laurent Orphée; one came to make sure that promise could never be kept.

## Host

Laurent Orphée, celebrated composer and clandestine signal writer; then Game Master

## Canonical solution

Victor was the counter-intelligence officer embedded in Laurent’s musical circle. He recognized the extraction code during rehearsal and killed Laurent before the complete suite could be heard. He then blamed a rights dispute. Separate musical, financial, and personal memories prove Victor understood both route and target too early.

## Truth timeline

1. **Music for one listener** — Laurent’s suite contains an extraction time and route intended for a defecting cryptographer.
2. **The unresolved cadence** — Conductor Victor Salvi killed Laurent because the final cadence identified Victor as the officer sent to stop the defection.
