_International financier_

Luxurious, amused, and never visibly surprised

The host privately promised: Vivienne promised to deliver a flawless double for one diplomatic appearance.

You planned to replace the minister permanently and control the office through an impostor.

Make the screen test look like Vivienne’s independent blackmail scheme.

## Secret
