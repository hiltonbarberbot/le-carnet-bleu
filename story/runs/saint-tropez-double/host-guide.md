# The Saint-Tropez Double

A film producer’s screen test is really an audition for a political impersonation.

## Fictional gathering

producer Vivienne Marchal's private screen test for a Riviera thriller called The Second Face brings five accomplished strangers to a house in Grambois. Each accepted a different private promise from Vivienne Marchal; one came to make sure that promise could never be kept.

## Host

Vivienne Marchal, film producer and covert talent scout; then Game Master

## Canonical solution

Dario financed the screen test to place his own operative as the minister’s double. Vivienne learned the substitution was intended to last beyond one public appearance and gathered witnesses. Dario killed her before the final test and exploited everyone’s film-industry secrets as alternate motives.

## Truth timeline

1. **The impossible casting** — Vivienne was recruiting a double for a visiting minister, not casting a film.
2. **The face behind the camera** — Dario Vescari killed Vivienne because she discovered he planned to replace the minister permanently.
