# Sylvie Caron

_Document restorer_

## Public face

Cultured, precise, and offended by vulgar suspicion

## Invitation

You were invited to honorary consul Alain Rochefort's farewell portrait sitting before his supposed retirement in your public capacity as document restorer.

The host privately promised: Alain promised to return the master seal you forged for him.

## Private identity

You manufactured Alain’s consular identity and dozens of passports beneath it.

## Private objective

Recover or discredit every trace of the master seal.

## Secret

You killed Alain when you learned the portrait sitting was an inquiry trap.

## Objectives

- **Collect the promise (1)** — Get another suspect to acknowledge what the host privately promised you: Alain promised to return the master seal you forged for him.
- **Gain useful leverage (2)** — Learn why Jonas Fèvre is concealing evidence and trade that knowledge for a clue, token, or public concession.
- **Escape the reckoning (3)** — Keep the final conviction away from you without inventing evidence.

## Relationships

- **velvet-consul-guest-2** — Jonas Fèvre can confirm part of your cover story, but only by exposing their own.
- **velvet-consul-guest-5** — Béatrice Lenoir once helped your career and now expects repayment.

## Evidence

- You saw photographer Jonas compare Alain’s face with a dead diplomat’s file before the gathering.
