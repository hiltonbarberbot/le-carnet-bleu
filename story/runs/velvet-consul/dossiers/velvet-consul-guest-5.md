# Béatrice Lenoir

_Portrait painter_

## Public face

Dreamy, candid, and unnervingly good at faces

## Invitation

You were invited to honorary consul Alain Rochefort's farewell portrait sitting before his supposed retirement in your public capacity as portrait painter.

The host privately promised: Alain promised to identify the unknown woman in your mother’s wartime sketchbook.

## Private identity

Your mother taught Sylvie the forgery technique visible in Alain’s papers.

## Private objective

Learn whether your mother created the first false consul or merely trained the culprit.

## Secret

You altered the commissioned portrait to include the dead diplomat’s profile.

## Objectives

- **Collect the promise (1)** — Get another suspect to acknowledge what the host privately promised you: Alain promised to identify the unknown woman in your mother’s wartime sketchbook.
- **Gain useful leverage (2)** — Learn why Sylvie Caron is concealing evidence and trade that knowledge for a clue, token, or public concession.
- **Shape the reckoning (3)** — Put your private objective before the table while keeping your own compromising secret from deciding the vote.

## Relationships

- **velvet-consul-guest-1** — Sylvie Caron can confirm part of your cover story, but only by exposing their own.
- **velvet-consul-guest-4** — Colonel Arthur Ward once helped your career and now expects repayment.

## Evidence

- You saw Sylvie’s restoration mark on a seal impression Jonas had secretly photographed.

## Opening cue

- **When the host calls on Béatrice Lenoir during the opening:** Unveil your sketch: Alain’s face layered over another man’s, and ask which identity is retiring.
