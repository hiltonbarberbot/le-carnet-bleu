# The Velvet Consul

A farewell portrait session becomes a contest over a forged diplomatic identity.

## Fictional gathering

honorary consul Alain Rochefort's farewell portrait sitting before his supposed retirement brings five accomplished strangers to a house in Grambois. Each accepted a different private promise from Alain Rochefort; one came to make sure that promise could never be kept.

## Host

Alain Rochefort, honorary consul and keeper of unofficial passports; then Game Master

## Canonical solution

Sylvie built Alain’s false diplomatic identity and feared the portrait archive would connect her to every passport it enabled. She poisoned him before the sitting and steered suspicion toward the photographer. The others independently place her at the origin of the forged seal and prove she knew the portrait’s hidden purpose.

## Truth timeline

1. **The consul who never was** — Alain’s title was a long-running intelligence cover built on the identity of a dead diplomat.
2. **The portrait’s final sitter** — Sylvie Caron killed Alain because his farewell portrait would expose her as the forger who created the cover.
