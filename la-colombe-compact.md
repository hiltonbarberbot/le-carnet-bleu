# La Colombe

*A blue diamond, seven guests, one dead host, and a road under water until morning.*

**7 players · 2 h 30 · Villa Mirabelle, Cap d’Antibes · August 1962**

## Read aloud

La Colombe—the Dove—is a blue diamond the size of a thumbnail, owned by the Vernay family for 140 years. Each August, Sacha de Vernay displays it under a glass dome at dinner.

This summer Sacha invited seven people, telling each: *Come on the fifteenth. I have something to announce that concerns you.* Rain has flooded the coast road and killed the telephone. At 7:30, the housekeeper found Sacha dead in his study. The glass dome is empty.

Nobody leaves before morning. By then you must discover who killed Sacha and who stole the Dove. They are not the same person.

## Host only: what happened

- **4:00:** Julien asks Sacha for 11,000 francs to leave for Marseille. Sacha refuses; they quarrel publicly.
- **4:30:** Sacha swims, leaving his keys in his linen jacket beside the pool. Rain begins at five.
- **5:15:** Hélène reads Sacha’s letter: Julien is his son by Odile, and inherits the villa and Dove. Sacha seals it in the study cabinet.
- **7:00:** Hélène serves Sacha cognac containing crushed heart pills. He dies.
- **7:10:** Renato enters to steal compromising photographs, finds Sacha dead, searches anyway and touches the glass.
- **7:15:** Hélène hides the pill bottle inside the hollow bronze dove on the salon mantelpiece.
- **7:20:** Odile finds the body, steals the diamond and hides it in a hollow book in the atelier for Julien. Poppy sees her cross the courtyard.
- **7:30:** Odile raises the alarm. Her missing ten minutes make her look guilty.

## Clues and movement

- **Keys:** Sacha’s wet jacket beside the pool.
- **Letter:** locked study cabinet; whoever finds it decides whether to read it aloud.
- **Diamond:** hollow art book in the atelier; its key is above the door.
- **Photograph:** taped behind the crooked portrait on the landing.
- **Pill bottle:** hollow bronze dove on the mantelpiece.
- **Money:** envelope under the unlit fireplace logs.
- **Cigarette box:** a player may secretly hide one object inside it once.

You may leave the room once per act, plus once during the blackout. A search takes two minutes; anyone present sees it.

## Evening

- **Drinks, 40 min:** Read the introduction and cards. Each player says their opening line.
- **Dinner, 60 min:** Lights out for three minutes; everyone may search one place. Eat, bargain and take people aside.
- **Coffee, 40 min:** The letter and Dove should surface. Everyone privately names the killer, thief and one exposed secret.
- **Reveal, 10 min:** Host reads the ending.

## Hélène de Vernay, 60
**Sacha’s wife · You killed him**

Your family’s money bought the villa, boat, cars and paintings; Sacha supplied the name and diamond. After 31 years enduring his affairs, you read that he planned to leave the villa and Dove to Julien. At seven you gave Sacha cognac containing his crushed heart pills and watched him drink.

You did not take the diamond. The incriminating bottle is inside the bronze dove. Sacha’s cabinet key is on his ring, but you do not know where the ring is. Renato left the study at 7:10, terrified. You also know he and Amaury have been lovers for six years.

**Do:** Find the keys through Julien or Odile; open the cabinet and burn the letter. Retrieve the bottle. Deny going upstairs—say you fetched a shawl. Build a truthful case against Renato. Make someone say aloud that the house is yours.

**Opening:** “Nobody is leaving, nobody is telephoning anybody, and that diamond did not walk off the table by itself.”

## Prince Amaury de Croy-Langeais, 45
**Royal · Dependent on Sacha**

You have a title, debts and no income. Sacha paid for your summers because a prince improved his guest list. You and Renato have secretly been together six years. Sacha owned photographs of you in Portofino and recently said, “Your face already hangs in my house.” Exposure ruins you both.

You saw Claude put something into Poppy’s coat. The landing portrait hangs crooked, Claude knows why, and the bronze dove rattles although it never did before.

**Do:** Search behind the portrait. Hide your relationship, even if Renato tries to confess. Attach yourself to whoever will own the villa. Trade the rattling-dove clue.

**Opening:** “I have no money, no alibi and no talent, so you may all stop looking at me like that.”

## Renato Sabbatini, 44
**Film star · Frightened witness**

Sacha kept compromising photographs of you and Amaury, using them to command your presence each summer. At 7:10 you entered the study to steal them. Sacha was already dead. You searched the desk, noticed an opened drawer and touched his glass.

At seven you passed Hélène carrying a glass upstairs like a waiter. Sacha once said of Poppy: “I’m not the one who owes that girl anything. Ask the minister.”

**Do:** Find the photographs through Amaury or Claude; search the cabinet, then the portrait. Hide your visit to the study until Hélène exposes it. Protect Amaury, even at your expense. Tell Poppy what Sacha said.

**Opening:** “I have known Sacha eleven years and never once been in this house without being paid for it. I will not pretend to cry.”

## Claude Mireau, 52
**Minister · Poppy’s father**

In 1939 you loved Margaret Ashcombe in Rouen; in 1940 she bore Poppy. You sent money until 1948, then stopped. Poppy does not know. You brought Sacha 40,000 francs, expecting blackmail, and hid it under the fireplace logs.

You feared Sacha’s letter named Poppy. Unable to confess, you slipped a three-line letter into her coat. Amaury saw you. While waiting on the landing, you noticed the small portrait hangs crooked.

**Do:** Learn what Sacha’s letter says before it is read publicly. Recover the money or prevent anyone lighting the fire. Decide whether to tell Poppy. Trade the crooked-portrait clue to Amaury or Renato.

**Opening:** “Whatever we settle tonight, we settle in this room.”

## Poppy Ashcombe, 22
**Singer · Seeking her father**

You came because Renato promised a diamond and because an anonymous letter said its writer knew your late mother in Rouen. You believe Sacha was your father. This afternoon you stole a photograph of your mother from his desk.

Before dinner you saw a woman in an apron cross the rainy courtyard carrying something in both hands. Julien secretly paints in the locked atelier and showed it to you. A letter has been slipped into your coat.

**Do:** Learn who your father was from Renato, then Odile. Search your coat and visit the atelier with Julien. Hide the stolen photograph. Do not let anyone frame Julien.

**Opening:** “I never met him. He wrote to me. I am the only person here with nothing to gain.”

## Julien Bassot, 27
**Boatman · Sacha’s unacknowledged son**

You were born in this house to Odile and have never known your father. At four you asked Sacha for 11,000 francs to leave for Marseille. He refused; you shouted, and several people heard. Nobody can vouch for you from six to 7:30.

Sacha’s keys are in his wet poolside jacket. You secretly paint in the old atelier; its key is above the door. Only Poppy knows. You are certain your mother has lied about your father.

**Do:** Get the keys. Learn what Sacha planned to announce. Make Odile name your father before a witness. Hide the quarrel as long as possible. If the pool or fire yields evidence, take possession of it.

**Opening:** “Ask where I was. Then ask which of you ever knew where I was on any other night.”

## Odile Bassot, 58
**Housekeeper · You stole the Dove**

You have run this house for 30 years. Julien is Sacha’s son; neither Julien nor anyone at dinner knows. This week Sacha finally wrote it down.

You found Sacha dead at 7:20. Before raising the alarm, you stole the Dove and hid it in a hollow book in the atelier so Julien would have something lawyers could not take. You did not know he used the room. Someone saw you cross the courtyard.

Sacha’s sealed letter is in the cabinet. You will reveal that only to Julien or someone promising to protect him. The bronze dove was empty when you polished it at six. You posted Claude’s money orders to Rouen and know he is Poppy’s father.

**Do:** Hide your missing ten minutes and the Dove; move it once if discovery seems likely. Find the keys and learn whether the letter survives. Get someone else to read it aloud. Protect Julien; confess to theft before he is accused of murder. Decide whether to tell him the truth.

**Opening:** “I will answer any question you ask me, and you may judge for yourselves what that is worth.”

---

## Reveal: read aloud

Sacha spent the week writing what he had never said: Julien, the boatman, was his son. He left Julien the villa and the Dove and planned to announce it at dinner.

Hélène had read the letter. The house was bought with her family’s money, and she would not lose it to the housekeeper’s boy. At seven she crushed Sacha’s heart pills into his cognac. The empty bottle was hidden inside the bronze dove.

At 7:10 Renato entered to steal photographs of himself and Amaury and found a dead man. He searched the room, but did not kill Sacha.

At 7:20 Odile found the body, stole the diamond and carried it through the rain to the atelier—for Julien, who did not know he was Sacha’s son. She was a thief, not a murderer.

Poppy also found her father: Claude, seated three places away and unable to look at her.

If Sacha’s letter was found and read, Julien inherits the villa and Dove. If it remained locked away, Hélène inherits everything. If at least four players accuse her correctly, she confesses—not about the pills, but about the money having been hers all along and nobody saying so for 30 years.
